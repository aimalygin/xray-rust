# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

Constructive technical disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks, harassment,
threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and publication of another person's
credentials or private information are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Keep security reports and sensitive configuration private.
- Respect maintainers' and contributors' time.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull
requests that violate these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently
ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.

Report abusive GitHub content with GitHub's built-in reporting tools. For
behavior elsewhere in project spaces, contact the repository owner through a
private channel listed on their GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive
personal details in an issue.
